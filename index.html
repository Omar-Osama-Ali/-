05 — FAQ

Common questions.

What services do you offer?

Brand identity and logo design, graphic design, social media design, product photography, web design and front-end development.

How do I start a project?

Send a short brief by email or WhatsApp. Include what you need, your goals, relevant references and any required deadline.

Can you build websites as well as design them?

Yes. I can work on the visual design and on front-end implementation using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Do you accept every project?

No. Project scope, timing, requirements and suitability are reviewed before work is accepted.

How are timelines and revisions handled?

They depend on the agreed scope. The deliverables, revision approach and expected schedule should be confirmed before work begins.
