07 — FAQ

Common questions.

What services do you offer?

Brand identity and logo design, graphic design, social media design, product photography, web design and front-end development.

How do I start a project?

Send a short brief by email or WhatsApp, or use the enquiry form below. Include what you need, your goals and any relevant references.

Can you design and build websites?

Yes. I can work on the visual design and on front-end implementation using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

What information should I include in a project enquiry?

What you need, the goal of the project, any references you like, your target deadline and anything specific the design needs to reflect.

How are timelines handled?

Timelines depend on the agreed scope and are confirmed before work begins, based on the deliverables involved.

How are revisions handled?

The number and structure of revisions is agreed as part of the project scope before work starts.

Do you accept every project?

No. Project scope, timing and suitability are reviewed first to make sure the work can be done well.

What happens after I contact you?

I'll reply to review the brief, ask any follow-up questions and confirm scope, timeline and next steps before work begins.
