Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information this website collects, why, and how it's handled. This is a general policy for a personal portfolio and enquiry form, not a substitute for legal advice. It should be reviewed against applicable Egyptian law and updated as the site or services change.

What this website collects

The only personal information collected directly by this website is what you choose to submit through the project enquiry form: your name, email address, a description of the project, and optionally a target deadline and a reference link.

Why it's collected

This information is used only to understand and respond to your enquiry. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

How enquiries are handled

Enquiry form submissions are sent to Omar Osama's email and, once a sending service is connected, processed through that service's infrastructure to deliver the message. Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond to and, where relevant, deliver the project.

Third-party communication services

Contacting Omar Osama through WhatsApp, LinkedIn, WeChat or email means that communication is also subject to that platform's own privacy policy. This website does not control how those services handle your data.

External links

This site links to external services such as LinkedIn, WhatsApp, WeChat, Instapay and a certificate verification page. Visiting those links is subject to the privacy practices of those third parties.

Tracking and analytics

This website does not use advertising trackers. If analytics are added in the future to understand general site usage, they will be disclosed here along with their purpose and any available controls.

Data minimization

The contact form only asks for what is needed to understand a project enquiry. It does not request passwords, government identification, payment card details or other sensitive personal data.

Your choices

You can ask for your enquiry information to be deleted at any time by emailing the address below.
